In Memory of
Chris Davage

Waterlooville Men's Shed

Biography

Chris Davage was a valued member of the Waterlooville community and a much–loved contributor to the Waterlooville Men's Shed. Born in 1947, Chris grew up with a natural talent for practical work, craftsmanship, and problem–solving — skills that would later make him an integral part of the Shed's early identity.

Chris joined the Waterlooville Men's Shed during its formative years and quickly became known for his friendly nature, steady reliability, and willingness to help anyone who needed a hand. Whether he was working with timber, repairing equipment, or supporting group projects, Chris brought patience, skill, and a quiet pride to everything he did. His presence in the workshop was a constant source of encouragement to newer members, and his practical knowledge helped shape the Shed's collaborative spirit.

Beyond his craftsmanship, Chris is remembered for his warmth and generosity. He had a way of making people feel welcome, and his calm, good–humoured approach to life made him a friend to many. Fellow members often recall his readiness to share advice, offer support, or simply enjoy a conversation over a cup of tea.
