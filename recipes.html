Sauce
BBQ Burger Sauce
MAYO BASE · NO COOK

Smoky, tangy and deeply savoury — the only burger sauce you’ll ever need.

Ingredients
  • ½ cupMayonnaise
  • 2 tbspBBQ sauce
  • 1 tbspDijon mustard
  • 1½ tspWorcestershire sauce
  • ½ tspSmoked paprika

Combine all ingredients and stir until smooth. Refrigerate until needed.

Sauce
Carolina Gold BBQ Sauce
MUSTARD BASE · SIMMER 5 MIN

Tangy mustard-based magic — pure BBQ gold from the Carolinas.

Ingredients
  • 1 cupYellow mustard
  • ¼ cupHoney
  • ¼ cupApple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbspKetchup
  • ¼ tspWorcestershire sauce
  • ¼ tspCayenne pepper

Whisk together and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Sauce
Bourbon Peppercorn Sauce
CREAM SAUCE · REDUCE TO THICKEN

Rich, creamy and deeply savoury — magnificent spooned over steak.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbspButter
  • 1Shallot, minced
  • 2 tbspBourbon or whiskey
  • ¾ cupBeef broth
  • ½ cupHeavy cream
  • 1 tbspWhole peppercorns, lightly crushed
  • 1 tspDijon mustard

Soften shallot in butter, deglaze with bourbon, add broth, cream and peppercorns. Reduce until thickened.

Spice Mix
Chili Powder Mix
DRY BLEND · MAKES ~4 TBSP

A well-rounded blend with warmth and depth — far better than anything out of a jar.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbspPaprika
  • 2 tspGround cumin
  • 1½ tspGarlic powder
  • 1 tspOnion powder
  • ½ tspDried oregano
  • ½ tspSmoked paprika
  • ½ tspCayenne pepper

Combine and store in an airtight jar. Use within 6 months for best flavour.

Dry Rub
BBQ Powder Mix
DRY RUB · GRILL OR SMOKER

Bold, smoky and savoury — adds instant BBQ flavour to anything you put on the grill.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbspPaprika
  • 2 tbspChili powder
  • 1 tbspBlack pepper
  • 1 tbspGranulated garlic
  • 1 tbspOnion powder
  • 2 tbspBrown sugar
  • 1 tspSalt

Mix well and store in a sealed container. Apply as a dry rub before grilling or smoking.
