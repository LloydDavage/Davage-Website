THE PROBLEM

Sound familiar?

Running a club's membership by hand is hard work

  • Spreadsheets everywhere

    The membership list lives on one volunteer's laptop, and nobody is sure which copy is current.

  • Chasing renewals

    Every year means reminder emails, bank statements and working out who has paid.

  • Worrying about data

    Members' personal details get passed around by email, and handing over to a new committee is painful.

Who it's for

Volunteer-run UK clubs and societies, such as sports clubs, archery clubs and hobby groups, run by a committee with a secretary, treasurer and membership secretary.

FEATURES

What it does

Every plan includes every feature

  • Member records

    One up-to-date list of members, with membership categories that match how your club works. Juniors are linked to their parents or guardians.

  • Online joining

    New members apply through an online form, and the committee approves them into the records.

  • Renewals

    Renewal reminders go out automatically, with a clear view of who is due, renewed or lapsed.

  • Payments

    Record membership payments (bank transfer, cash, cheque or card) and see at a glance who has paid.

  • Members' area

    Members sign in to see their membership, renewal date and payments, update their own details, and see their juniors' memberships. Each has an on-screen membership card.

  • Email your members

    Send club news to everyone or a group, with unsubscribes handled automatically.

  • Committee roles

    The secretary, treasurer and membership secretary each get access to just what they need.

  • Reports and exports

    Download your data whenever you need it. It's your club's data.

  • Audit log

    See who changed what and when, so handovers between committees are painless.

  • Secure sign-in

    Two-factor authentication for everyone with committee access.

Coming later: an events calendar with sign-ups, range or session booking, and a members' marketplace.

Your members' data, looked after

  • Hosted in the UK

    Built with UK GDPR in mind. Read the MyClubSorted privacy notice.

  • Each club's data kept separate

    Every club has its own database with its own access credentials, so no club's records can ever be seen by another.
