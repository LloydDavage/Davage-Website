About

Lloyd Davage is a Senior Solutions Architect with over 19 years of experience delivering enterprise technology solutions across the energy and finance sectors. Currently at SSE Energy Markets, he leads DevOps strategy and cloud-native architecture across multiple value streams, with a focus on platform engineering, API integration, and secure-by-design practices.

Prior to SSE, Lloyd held senior architecture and engineering roles at Shell across Finance, Treasury, and Trading & Shipping — contributing to high-profile programmes including a VP-award-winning Quantum Upgrade project (2023) and a Treasury Innovation Award-winning Splunk implementation (2019). His technical depth spans .NET and C# application development, Oracle and SQL Server databases, Azure infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and Terraform-based infrastructure-as-code, complemented by a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Portsmouth and a portfolio of professional certifications.

Beyond his corporate career, Lloyd is a committed community contributor. He co-founded Ordnance Survey Archers in 2019, where he serves as Secretary and a qualified Archery GB Level 2 Coach, helping grow the club to over 50 members. He also serves as Secretary and a qualified NSRA Range Officer at Southampton Small-bore Rifle & Pistol Club, where he built the club's online management system for membership, bookings, and competitions. His precision sports background — competitive archery and fullbore rifle shooting at Bisley ranges — reflects the same discipline, attention to detail, and performance-under-pressure mindset he brings to his professional work. His making interests extend into 3D printing and design using a Creality Ender S1 and SolidWorks, CNC routing, electronics projects, and developing websites for shooting and archery organisations across the south of England.
